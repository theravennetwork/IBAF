About Us

Who We Are

The Islamic Burial Assistance Foundation (IBAF) is a humanitarian organization dedicated to supporting vulnerable individuals and families through dignified burial assistance, emergency support, and community-based interventions, in line with Islamic values and applicable laws.

Our ambition is to build a trusted humanitarian institution that delivers meaningful assistance, honors human dignity, and strengthens community solidarity across Nigeria.

Read More About Us

Our Vision رؤيتنا

A compassionate and dignified society where vulnerable individuals and families receive dignified support, guided by Islamic values and a shared commitment to humanity.

Our Mission رسالتنا

To provide and facilitate dignified burial assistance, emergency humanitarian support, and community-based services through responsible resource management, strategic partnerships, and compassionate service.

Our Core Values • قيمنا

Compassion الرحمة

Serving others with heartfelt kindness, mercy, and deep understanding during distress.

Human Dignity الكرامة الإنسانية

Treating every single deceased and bereaved person with unyielding sacred respect.

Integrity الأمانة

Managing responsibilities, public trusts, and charitable donations with absolute honesty.

Justice العدل

Upholding equitable assistance without bias, prioritizing the most vulnerable households.

Accountability المساءلة

Rigorous reporting, transparent accounting, and full compliance with Islamic jurisprudence.

Solidarity التضامن

Uniting communities to ensure no grieving family ever faces burial costs alone.

Governance & Trust

Built on Trust. Guided by Responsibility.

IBAF recognizes that public trust is essential to effective humanitarian service. We are committed to strengthening our governance, financial controls, ethical conduct, and accountability systems.

Responsible Leadership

Clear institutional responsibilities, active trustee oversight, and accountable decision-making at every operational tier.

Financial Accountability

Rigorous donation segregation, dual-signatory approvals, independent auditing, and strict adherence to SCUML/AML guidelines.

Beneficiary Protection

Protecting the sacred privacy, dignity, and personal safety of grieving households under NDPR data compliance.

Islamic Ethics

Continuous supervision by respected Islamic scholars ensuring every disbursement conforms to authentic Quranic principles.

Institutional Transparency

Publishing verified program metrics, operational expense breakdowns, and vehicle deployment logs to all stakeholders.

Complaints & Feedback

Providing transparent, confidential channels for concerns, inquiries, or grievances to be investigated fairly.

Our Uncompromising Commitment

We ensure that compassion is always supported by sound governance, ethical conduct, and responsible stewardship.

Partners & Donors

Together, We Can Do More

Humanitarian challenges require collaboration, shared responsibility, and a steadfast commitment to meaningful action across healthcare and community institutions.

Healthcare & Hospital Alliances

Partnering with hospital social welfare units in Abuja and across Nigeria to swiftly identify destitute families facing burial distress.

Emergency Fleet Logistics

Collaborating with automotive service providers, fuel partners, and medical equipment distributors to keep ambulances operational 24/7.

Technical & Professional Pro-Bono

Enlisting legal experts, audit firms, software engineers, and medical professionals offering pro-bono institutional backing.

Joint Humanitarian Projects

Co-developing long-term Waqf cemetery facilities, solar-powered morgue logistics, and bereavement hardship relief reserves.
